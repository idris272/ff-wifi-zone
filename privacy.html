1. Who we are

F&F WiFi Zone ("we", "us") sells WiFi vouchers at Bole Hill Top, Bole, Savannah Region, Ghana. We decide how the personal information described here is used. The business is operated by Cotton Scents Enterprise.

2. What we collect

When you buy a voucher. Your name, the mobile money number and network you paid from, the package you chose, and the payment details you send us (such as the amount, time and transaction reference). The order form on our website does not send this to a server. It builds a WhatsApp message that you choose to send to us.

When you pay. Mobile money payments show us the sender's name, number, amount and time in our MoMo records and SMS. If online payment through Paystack is offered, Paystack collects and processes your payment details itself. We do not see or store your card details or your mobile money PIN.

When you use our WiFi. Our hotspot system records your voucher code, your device's address (MAC address), the time you connect and disconnect, and how much data you use. We do not read the content of your messages or the pages you visit.

When you visit this website. We do not use accounts, advertising trackers or analytics, and we do not set our own cookies. Our hosting provider, Vercel, may keep standard technical logs such as your IP address and browser type to run and protect the site. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

When you message us. If you contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy apply to that conversation, and we keep the messages you send us.

3. Why we use it

  • To confirm your payment and send you your voucher.
  • To give you internet access and make sure a voucher is used only as paid for.
  • To answer your questions, fix problems and process refunds.
  • To keep the network secure and stop misuse.
  • To keep records and to follow the law, including responding to lawful requests from the authorities.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We only share information where we need to:

  • Payment providers such as your mobile money network and Paystack, to take and confirm payments.
  • Service providers that help us run the service, such as Vercel for website hosting and WhatsApp for messaging.
  • The authorities, when the law requires us to or to protect people from harm.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for about 12 months so we can handle refunds and disputes. We keep WiFi connection records for about 90 days. After that we delete them or remove your personal details, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

6. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting who can see our records. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

7. Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you have rights over your personal information. You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it or stop using it where the law allows. To do this, contact us using the details below. If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

8. Children

Our website is not aimed at children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place the order. If you think a child has given us personal information without a parent's knowledge, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
