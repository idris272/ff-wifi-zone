Questions people ask.

What does unlimited mean?

There is no data cap on any package. Speeds go up to 50 Mbps down and 20 Mbps up. The speed you see can change with how many people are online at the same time.

Which MoMo networks can I pay with?

MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and AirtelTigo Money. Pick your network in the Buy form and we show you the right dial code.

How do I get my voucher after paying?

Tap "I've paid" in the Buy form. It opens WhatsApp with your order already written. Send it and we reply with your voucher code.

What does the voucher code do?

It logs you in. Connect to the F&F WiFi Zone network, wait for the login page, type in your code and you are online.

Where are you?

Bole Hill Top. Look for the F&F WiFi Zone network on your phone or laptop.
